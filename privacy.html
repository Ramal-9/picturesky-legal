Terms & Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Who we are

Picturesky (“we”, “the app”) is a mobile messenger. This page explains what data we process, why we do it, and the rules for using the app. By creating an account you agree to these terms and this policy.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, username and password. The password is handled by Firebase Authentication and we never see it in plain text. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from them.
  • Content you send: messages, photos, videos, voice messages, your profile picture and chat settings.
  • Calls: to connect voice and video calls our server processes connection data such as IP addresses and call signals. We do not record calls.
  • Device: a push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver message and call notifications.
  • Usage and diagnostics: we use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to find crashes.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: video calls, taking photos and videos.
  • Microphone: calls and voice messages.
  • Photo library: choosing photos and videos to send.

We do not access your contacts or your location. People find each other by username. Permissions are requested only when you use the related feature, and you can withdraw them in your phone settings.

4. How we use data

To create your account, let you message and call other people, deliver notifications, keep the service secure and prevent abuse, improve the app, and comply with the law. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data.

5. Where data is stored

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Analytics): account data and messages.
  • Our own file storage: photos, videos and voice messages.
  • Our own server: call connection and push notifications.

These providers process data only to run the app.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (TLS) and protected by access rules. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: they are stored on our servers so they can sync between your devices, so the service operator can technically access stored data. We do not read private messages as a routine matter. We may access content only to investigate abuse reports, fix technical problems, or when required by law. No system is perfectly secure.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Other users see your name, username, profile picture and the content you send to them. We share data with the providers listed above only to operate the app, and with authorities only when the law requires it.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app settings, and all data associated with your account is deleted. You can also request deletion by email (see Contact).

9. Age

You must be at least 13 years old to use Picturesky. If you believe a child under 13 uses the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

10. Your rights

Depending on your country, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data. Write to us and we will respond.

11. Terms of use

  • You are responsible for your account and password.
  • Do not use the app for anything illegal, for harassment, hate, spam or content that harms others. Do not impersonate other people.
  • You own your content. You allow us to store it and deliver it to the people you choose, only to run the service.
  • We may suspend or delete accounts that break these rules.
  • The app is provided “as is”. We do our best, but we cannot guarantee uninterrupted service.

We may update this page. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use the app means you accept the changes.
